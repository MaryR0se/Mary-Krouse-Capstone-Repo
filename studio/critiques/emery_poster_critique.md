# Week 12 Draft Poster Critique
## Strong points:

* I do really enjoy the general vibe of the template you're using, it feels very fitting!
* You do a great job at condensing a lot of information down into a readable format, especially in the data engineering and data sources sections.
* Your motivation is really strong and overall you do a good job linking this project to issues that are currently affecting all of us.

## Recommendations:

* You might want to more explicitly mention that you're using the IARP's data outside of just one bullet point in the data sources section - the framing of the rest of the poster makes it seem like you're mostly relying on your own survey (which could very well be the case and it's just harder to tell without the results in there yet lol)
* If possible, you might want to rearrange the template a little bit - it's great that you have so much room for results/etc but the placement of that section makes the flow sort of strange. In particular, right now the data sources section is the last thing I would read if I was scanning through the poster, which likely will not aid people in interpreting your data and conclusions. Without moving the largest section, my suggestion would be to put the data sections where related work currently is, put ethics/limitations where the data sections currently are, and put related work above ethics/limitations, but it would perhaps be ideal to concentrate the large section more to the right side of the poster.
