# Week 6 Proposal Critique

## Strong points:

* It's clear that you have a specific vision for this project and its end product; each of the integration pillars given are quite specific and actionable.
* I appreciate the definitions given at the beginning - they provided helpful context for the project as an outsider to the furry community.
* I think that your ethics section is strong and shows a lot of thought, especially when you acknowledge the drawbacks of certain ethical decisions (i.e. not collecting identifiable information means people may be able to submit multiple responses). 


## Recommendations:

* I think you may be aiming for slightly too broad of a scope for an individual project. You list four different final deliverables (poster, write-up, slideshow/talk, and data art) but it seems like you'll have very little time to put these finished products together given that a significant chunk of the semester will be spent solely collecting your own data. 
* Something you're missing right now in your introduction/context/impact is a connection to broader research. Looking for previous similar projects would potentially also help you get more specific about the impact - especially if there is no pre-existing research, in which case this could serve as a preliminary study.
* I would suggest potentially using the questions you have listed under the machine learning section as your research subquestions, as they are more specific and actionable than the current main question about homophobia and sentiment towards furries.
